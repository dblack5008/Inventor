
VOLTAGE REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY FOR RF STAGE 1
===========================================

PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT:
This summary identifies all unique voltage supplies needed to power components in this system.

DUAL-RAIL COMPONENTS:
Some components require TWO voltage supplies (both positive and negative):
  - TTL-1SP8T-183: Requires +5V AND -5V
  - ZX76-31A-PNS+: Requires +3V AND -3.3V  
  - ZX76-24G-30-S+: Requires +3.3V AND -3V

WHY "RAIL 1" AND "RAIL 2"?
These columns exist because some components need multiple voltage supplies.
If a component only needs one voltage, only Rail 1 is populated.
If it needs two voltages, both Rail 1 and Rail 2 are used.
